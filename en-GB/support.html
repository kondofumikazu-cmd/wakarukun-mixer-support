iPhone / iPad · iOS / iPadOS 26.2+

KF transcription mixer turns speech from the built-in microphone on your iPhone or iPad into live captions for meetings and talks. Choose a spoken language and tap Start.

Adjust caption size from 100% to 200%, show reading guides and phonetics, and tap originals to edit them. Apple Translation displays translations alongside the original. Rotate 180 degrees to face another person; supported translation language pairs can be swapped.

Share your captions or view someone else's using a QR code or link, in the app or a browser. Issue a QR before an event, give it a title, and start sharing later from the QR history. Viewers cannot edit the source captions. Reading guides and text size can be changed on their own screen.

Save shared captions from the beginning of the sharing session to your own saved history. After you choose Save, received final results and corrections update that copy. Catching up requires an active session. Captions never received before an offline session ends cannot be recovered.

Edit titles and originals in saved history, update translations or delete items. iCloud sync for saved captions and user dictionaries is on by default and can be turned off. It uses the same Apple Account and iCloud Drive. Local saving remains available.

External recognition is preferred by default. Audio and recognition vocabulary may be sent to Apple. If network recognition is unavailable, the app switches to on-device recognition with language data already installed. Selecting on-device recognition does not automatically enable external recognition. Download languages in advance. Recognition and translation can contain errors.

Share captions by QR or link in the app or a browser. Captions, available translations, speaker names and titles are end-to-end encrypted on your device before Cloudflare relays them. Decryption secrets are not sent to the relay. Audio and pre-existing saved histories are not sent. The browser viewer is served by GitHub Pages. Link holders can read and save copies that cannot be recalled. Reissue older QR codes and update both sending and viewing apps.

On supported devices, Apple Intelligence can summarize saved captions on-device. A compatible OS and language and enabled Apple Intelligence are required. Check generated results against the original.

Supported built-in microphones offer Narrow and Wide pickup. You can listen through supported speaker, Bluetooth or wired outputs and continue active transcription in the background. The input is the built-in microphone.

There are no ads, tracking, analytics or app-specific accounts. The interface supports 50 languages and regions.

Apple Vision Pro · visionOS 26+

KF transcription mixer displays speech from the built-in microphone on Apple Vision Pro in a floating caption panel. Use it as a window in the Shared Space, and use standard visionOS controls to place and resize the panel for comfortable reading. There is one microphone input.

Use captions, text-size controls, reading guides and saved histories to follow classes, lectures and meetings at your own pace. These tools can support people who find audio alone difficult to follow, including people with hearing loss, ADHD or learning disabilities.

Enter or edit the meeting title directly at the top of the main caption panel. In Settings, choose the spoken language, adjust the caption text size, or turn on Japanese kana reading guides. Select Start to transcribe and Stop to finish. Select caption text to edit the original. User speech-recognition dictionaries are also available.

Your captions and captions received through a sharing link are saved automatically on your device with their title. Final results and corrections received during viewing update the history. Open Saved captions to view, edit originals or delete items. No manual Save captions action is needed. iCloud Drive history sync is on by default. Turn it off in Saved captions to use local storage only. Sync requires your Apple Account and iCloud Drive.

On supported devices, Apple Intelligence summarizes saved captions on-device in their original language. Summaries support reading guides. A compatible OS and language and enabled Apple Intelligence are required. Check generated results against the original.

Share captions by QR or link. Your device end-to-end encrypts caption content before Cloudflare relays it; the decryption secret is not sent to the relay. Audio and pre-existing histories are not sent. The browser viewer uses GitHub Pages. Providers can see connection metadata. Sharing ends on Stop, expiry (at most eight hours) or publisher interruption. Link holders can save copies that cannot be recalled. Reissue older QR codes and update both native apps. On Apple Vision Pro, open a QR image or enter a link without a camera. Adjust text size and reading guides in the viewer.

On-device speech recognition is selected by default. You can instead choose external recognition. On-device recognition processes speech on your device and never automatically switches to external recognition. External recognition may send audio to Apple with your permission and requires an internet connection. The first download of speech-recognition language data may also require internet access. Transcription stops when the app moves to the background.

Automatic punctuation is supported. Results depend on the OS, language and recognition method. Speech recognition and phonetic annotations can be inaccurate. This visionOS version does not create new translations; saved translations and translations received through sharing remain readable.

There are no ads, tracking, analytics or app-specific accounts. The interface supports 50 languages and regions.

Privacy Policy

Wakarukun Mixer processes microphone audio locally on your Mac. Audio, participant names, settings and recordings are not sent to the developer or to external servers. The app does not use advertising, tracking or analytics.

Microphone access is used for input monitoring, calibration and audio processing. You can change permission in System Settings > Privacy & Security > Microphone.

Participant names, microphone assignments and calibration data are stored locally in the Wakarukun Mixer app container. Recording is optional. When you start a recording, processed audio and settings information are saved in your chosen folder.

You can delete saved recordings in Finder and edit participant names and placement notes in Microphone Settings. Local app settings remain on your Mac until you remove the app's data.

If you contact support, choose only the information needed for your request. Check whether recordings or participant information are included before sharing them.

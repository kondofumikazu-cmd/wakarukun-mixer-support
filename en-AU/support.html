iPhone / iPad · iOS / iPadOS 26.2+

KF transcription mixer turns speech from the built-in microphone on your iPhone or iPad into live captions for meetings and talks. Choose a spoken language and tap Start.

Adjust caption size from 100% to 200%, show reading guides and phonetics, and tap originals to edit them. Apple Translation displays translations alongside the original. Rotate 180 degrees to face another person; supported translation language pairs can be swapped.

Share your captions or view someone else's using a QR code or link, in the app or a browser. Issue a QR before an event, give it a title, and start sharing later from the QR history. Viewers cannot edit the source captions. Reading guides and text size can be changed on their own screen.

Save shared captions from the beginning of the sharing session to your own saved history. After you choose Save, received final results and corrections update that copy. Catching up requires an active session. Captions never received before an offline session ends cannot be recovered.

Edit titles and originals in saved history, update translations or delete items. iCloud sync for saved captions and user dictionaries is on by default and can be turned off. It uses the same Apple Account and iCloud Drive. Local saving remains available.

External recognition is preferred by default. Audio and recognition vocabulary may be sent to Apple. If network recognition is unavailable, the app switches to on-device recognition with language data already installed. Selecting on-device recognition does not automatically enable external recognition. Download languages in advance. Recognition and translation can contain errors.

Only when sharing is started, new captions, translations, speaker names, title, languages, timestamps and reading guides are sent to a Cloudflare relay. Audio and pre-existing saved histories are not sent. Caption text is held in server memory during the session and cleared on stop, expiry or termination after a sending interruption. Sessions last at most eight hours. Anyone with the viewing link can view and save; copies saved by others cannot be recalled. Sharing requires a connection.

Supported built-in microphones offer Narrow and Wide pickup. You can listen through supported speaker, Bluetooth or wired outputs and continue active transcription in the background. The input is the built-in microphone.

There are no ads, tracking, analytics or app-specific accounts. The interface supports 50 languages and regions.

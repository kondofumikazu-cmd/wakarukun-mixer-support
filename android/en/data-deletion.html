Accounts

KF transcription mixer does not create an app-specific account. A Google account is used only for optional Drive sync. Manage local, synced and shared data separately using these steps.

Delete individual items

  1. Delete a caption from saved captions, a dictionary entry from the dictionary, or an issued QR from QR history.
  2. If Drive sync is enabled, delete captions and dictionary entries while connected and confirm that Sync now completes. Other devices apply the deletion after their next successful sync.
  3. Content-free deletion records and sync identifiers may remain to prevent deleted items from returning. To remove the entire synced store, follow the next section.

Delete all Google Drive sync data

  1. Stop sync on every device using that account to prevent re-upload. Export anything you want to keep first.
  2. Open Google Drive settings in a browser, choose Manage apps and locate this app.
  3. Under Options, choose Delete hidden app data and confirm. Google's labels may vary by language and interface.
  4. If you no longer need access, disconnect the Google account in the app or remove its permission in Google account connections. Revoking access alone does not erase previously stored Drive data.
  5. This removes the app's sync store, not local data or files you manually exported to Drive. Delete those separately. Re-enabling sync while local content remains may upload it again.

Clear all local app data

Stop sharing and finish any Drive deletion first. Use your device's app information settings to clear this app's storage/data, or uninstall it in a way that removes its app data. Settings vary across Android devices and Chromebooks.

Delete exported files separately using the file manager or their chosen destination. Clearing local data does not remove Drive data, recipients' copies or external speech-service records.

Live sharing and recipients' copies

Stopping sharing removes the relay's session data. If you cannot stop manually, sharing lasts at most eight hours and ends within 90 seconds of interrupted publishing. The publisher cannot remove copies or screenshots saved by recipients; ask the recipient directly.

External services and support correspondence

Manage external speech and SDK information through the providers' account settings and privacy policies. The developer cannot directly delete your Drive store or speech-service records through a support request.

For deletion of support correspondence or help with these steps, email kondofumikazu@icloud.com and identify the relevant correspondence with minimal information, such as its date. Do not send passwords, authorization codes or actual audio/caption content.

Android and Chromebook

KF transcription mixer displays, edits, saves, translates and shares captions on Android phones, tablets and compatible Chromebooks. The application ID is jp.fumifumi.WakarukunMixer.

This is a support page, not an announcement of Google Play availability.

Speech recognition does not start

  • Allow microphone access in device settings, and check the microphone and language selected in the app.
  • Open the speech language downloads screen and wait for the selected model to finish downloading. If in-app downloads are unavailable, open speech settings, select Google Voice Typing in keyboard settings, and add an offline language. A scheduled download is not yet a ready model.
  • The on-device preference uses Google's classic speech recognition service. Language support depends on the Google service and downloaded models on your device. This preference does not completely prohibit network use. Choosing external recognition may also transmit audio to that service.
  • Multiple inputs on Chromebooks and Bluetooth support depend on the device, OS and peripherals; all combinations are not guaranteed.

Prepare translation

Use translation language downloads in the audio settings to prepare models. The first download requires a connection; translation then runs on your device. Recognition and translation can be inaccurate. Confirm important content with the original or the speaker.

Sync saved captions and dictionaries

Open saved captions, then Google Drive, and connect a Google account. Check the account and grant access. Use Sync now to check the result. Local saving remains available when Drive cannot connect.

Disconnecting an account is separate from deleting its Drive data. See data deletion instructions.

Share captions

Issue a QR or viewing link and start sharing. Issuing a QR works offline; publishing and receiving captions require a connection. Give the link only to intended recipients.

Starting calibrated microphones on supported devices relays encrypted comparison audio among up to eight devices in the same QR room over the internet. The devices can use different Wi-Fi networks or mobile data. Comparison audio is not saved or sent to view-only devices.

Exported .fumicap files can be imported by compatible versions of the app. Recipients' copies are separate from the source.

Contact support

Email kondofumikazu@icloud.com with the app version, device, OS version and steps that cause the problem. Do not send actual audio or caption content, passwords or authorization codes.

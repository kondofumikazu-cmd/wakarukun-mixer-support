Scope and provider

This policy covers KF transcription mixer for Android and Chromebook, provided by Fumikazu Kondo. Its application ID is jp.fumifumi.WakarukunMixer. The app does not create an app-specific account. Optional Google Drive sync requires a Google account and your authorization.

See the Apple privacy policy for the Apple versions.

Audio and speech recognition

Microphone audio is used for audio processing and caption creation. Google's classic speech recognition is the default and prefers downloaded on-device models. Preparing language models may require a download. The offline preference depends on the recognition service and does not completely prohibit network use.

If you select external recognition, audio and recognition hints may be sent to the speech service configured on your device, such as Google. Its settings and policies govern that processing and retention. Comparison audio from calibrated microphones started during sharing uses the separate encrypted relay path described below.

Local captions, dictionaries and settings

The app stores saved originals, translations, titles, speaker names, languages, timestamps, reading guides, dictionary entries, settings and issued QR history on your device. Sync identifiers and change records support editing, conflicts and deletion. Captions can contain personal information.

Ordinary use does not require your name, address, phone number, contact list or location. The developer does not collect your captions or audio for AI training.

Translation and Google ML Kit

Translation runs on your device after the required ML Kit models are downloaded. ML Kit does not send translation inputs or outputs to Google. Model updates and QR scanning modules require network access.

ML Kit sends API performance and usage metrics to Google for maintenance, improvement and abuse detection. The app has no developer-operated advertising, behavioral tracking or analytics, but Google SDK diagnostics still apply. See ML Kit Terms & Privacy.

Optional Google Drive sync

After you authorize a Google account, the app reads and writes saved captions and user dictionaries in that account's private app data folder. This includes caption text, translations, titles, speaker names, timestamps, readings, dictionary entries, languages, conflicts and deletion records. Audio and issued QR history are not synced.

The requested Drive scope is drive.appdata; access to unrelated files in My Drive is not requested. The app retrieves an account identifier and email address to identify and display the selected account. Authorization tokens are handled through Google's device authorization service and are not included in caption files or relay requests.

Requests use HTTPS. Drive storage is separate from the end-to-end encryption used for live caption sharing. Google's terms govern data stored by Drive. Stopping sync or disconnecting an account does not automatically erase local or previously uploaded data.

Our handling of Google API data follows Google's Limited Use rules and the Google API Services User Data Policy. Account data is not sold or used for advertising or developer AI training.

Optional live caption sharing

Sharing starts only when you choose to start it. New caption text, translations, speaker names, title, language, timestamps and readings are encrypted on your device with AES-256-GCM before being sent to a Cloudflare relay. Earlier saved histories, the full dictionary and settings are not sent. The decryption secret stays with client devices and the viewing link, not the relay.

When you start a calibrated microphone during sharing, up to eight supported devices in the same QR room relay end-to-end encrypted comparison audio over the internet to reduce duplicate recognition. A shared Wi-Fi network is not required, and Wi-Fi or mobile data is used. Comparison audio is processed only in short memory buffers; it is not saved to files or Drive, added to history or replayed. View-only devices do not receive comparison audio. Transmission stops when the microphone or sharing stops, and local transcription continues when comparison is unavailable.

The relay holds the latest caption-display ciphertext in memory. It does not write caption or comparison-audio content, ciphertext or decryption keys to disk. It stores credential hashes, a session generation, expiry and last publishing time for connection management. Sessions last at most eight hours and end on Stop, expiry, or within 90 seconds of interrupted publishing; relay data and session metadata are then removed.

Anyone holding the link or QR can read captions and save a copy. Saved copies and screenshots cannot be recalled. The browser viewer is served by GitHub Pages and decrypts on the viewer's device. Inform participants and obtain any necessary consent before sharing.

Cloudflare and GitHub may process connection metadata such as IP addresses and access times under their own policies. Content encryption does not anonymize connection metadata.

Files, scanning and permissions

Exported .fumicap files contain titles, caption text, translations, speakers, timestamps and readings, without audio or the full dictionary. The app does not encrypt these exported files. You choose the destination and recipients; their services' terms apply.

QR scanning uses Google's scanning interface or an image you select. The developer does not receive the image. Microphone, connected audio device and notification permissions support their corresponding features and can be changed in device settings.

Retention and deletion

Local captions, dictionary entries and QR history remain until you delete them or clear the app's data. When sync is enabled, caption and dictionary deletions propagate after successful communication. Content-free deletion markers and sync identifiers may remain to prevent deleted items from returning.

Uninstalling the app alone does not erase Drive data, exported files or recipients' copies. See data deletion instructions. External speech services and SDK providers control their own retention through their settings and policies.

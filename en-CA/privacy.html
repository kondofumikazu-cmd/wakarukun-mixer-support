Privacy Policy

2026-10-22

This policy applies to KF transcription mixer on iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple Vision Pro. The app provides audio processing, caption creation, editing and saving, optional caption sharing, and Apple speech recognition, translation and iCloud services. No app-specific account is required.

Apple network speech recognition is the default on iPhone and iPad. Audio and recognition vocabulary may be sent to Apple. If network recognition is unavailable, prepared on-device recognition is used. Choosing on-device recognition does not automatically enable external recognition. Apple recognition, translation and language downloads follow Apple service terms.

Starting optional caption sharing end-to-end encrypts new originals, translations, speaker names, title, languages, timestamps and reading guides on your device using AES-256-GCM before sending them to a Cloudflare relay. Audio, pre-existing saved histories, the full dictionary and settings are not sent. The decryption secret stays on client devices and in the viewing link; it is not sent to the relay. Sharing starts only on your explicit action.

The publishing device retains the full active session and resends it encrypted for reconnection and late joining. The relay holds only the latest ciphertext snapshot in memory and does not write caption text, ciphertext or decryption secrets to disk. Sharing lasts at most eight hours and ends on Stop, expiry, or within 90 seconds of publisher interruption, clearing relay memory and connection-management hashes, expiry and session data.

Anyone with the QR or viewing link can read captions and save a copy. Viewers cannot edit the source; copies and screenshots cannot be recalled. HTTPS/WSS also protects transport. The browser viewer is served separately from Cloudflare by GitHub Pages and decrypts on the viewing device. Protection depends on trusted apps, the viewer page and endpoint devices, so share links only with trusted people. Reissue QR codes and links from older versions.

Cloudflare processes relay connection information such as IP addresses, connection times and traffic sizes. GitHub may process IP addresses and related information when serving public pages. Content encryption does not anonymize this network metadata. The app uses no advertising, tracking or analytics; relay code does not log caption text, decryption secrets or authentication credentials. Infrastructure processing, retention and deletion follow the Cloudflare and GitHub privacy policies.

Caption sharing data is used only to provide app functionality. Speaker names and captions may identify people. It is not used for advertising, tracking, behavioral analytics, data sales or developer collection of training data.

iCloud sync for saved captions and user dictionaries is on by default and can be turned off in the app. Saved originals, translations, titles, speaker names and times, plus dictionary words, readings, languages and deletion information, are stored in iCloud Drive for the same Apple Account. Audio and recordings are not synced. iCloud retention and deletion follow Apple services and account settings. Local saving remains available.

On supported devices, Apple Intelligence uses an on-device model to summarize saved captions. Summarization does not send captions to the developer or an external AI service. Generated output may be incorrect. Summaries are saved locally and, when sync is enabled, shared through iCloud Drive for the same Apple Account. Delete saved summaries and their source captions in the app.

Settings and issued QR history are stored on your device. Issuing a QR in advance does not send captions. Stop sharing, delete saved captions, dictionary entries or QR history, and change iCloud sync in the app.

Before starting sharing, tell conversation participants that captions will be relayed and shared and obtain any necessary consent. Stopping sharing cannot erase copies others have saved. Captions not received before an offline session ends cannot be recovered.

On versions with recording, such as Mac, processed audio and settings are saved to the chosen location only when you start recording. Delete recordings with the device file manager. Microphone, speech recognition and camera permissions can be changed in device settings. The camera is used when you choose to scan a QR code.

When contacting support, send only the necessary information. Contact the address below about data handling. Changes to this policy will be indicated by an updated date on this page.

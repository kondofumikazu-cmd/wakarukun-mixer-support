Support

Choose a microphone and input channel for each participant. Start capture to see input levels. For multiple independent microphones, measure background sound and let each participant speak alone near their microphone for five seconds to calibrate. Recalibrate after changing placement or gain. Use headphones for live output.

Enable captions and choose the spoken language. Manage user dictionaries on the first screen and select a dictionary for each participant. The reading-guide switch controls furigana and other supported pronunciation guides. Apple Translation requires macOS 15 or later.

Save captions with a title, then open them from saved history. Optional iCloud synchronization requires the same Apple Account and iCloud Drive on your devices. Use the app's synchronization controls to enable synchronization or synchronize manually. Local use remains available when synchronization is off or unavailable. Check microphone permission and the internet connection if recognition cannot start.

Share captions by QR or link in the app or a browser. Captions, available translations, speaker names and titles are end-to-end encrypted on your device before Cloudflare relays them. Decryption secrets are not sent to the relay. Audio and pre-existing saved histories are not sent. The browser viewer is served by GitHub Pages. Sessions last at most eight hours; relay data is cleared on stop, expiry or termination after a sending interruption. Providers process connection information such as IP addresses. Link holders can read and save copies that cannot be recalled. Reissue older QR codes and update both sending and viewing apps.

On supported devices, Apple Intelligence can summarize saved captions on-device. A compatible OS and language and enabled Apple Intelligence are required. Check generated results against the original.

Share saved captions as .fumicap files through AirDrop, Mail or other system share destinations. Files preserve titles, originals, translations, speakers, times and reading guides, without audio or the full dictionary. Preview received files and save independent histories without changing the source. These files are not encrypted by this app; share only with trusted recipients.

iPhone / iPad · iOS / iPadOS 26.2+

KF transcription mixer turns the built-in microphone on iPhone/iPad into captions for meetings and talks. Choose a language and tap Start.

Adjust caption size from 100% to 200%, show reading guides, and tap originals to edit. Apple Translation displays translations after completed sentences while keeping earlier translations visible. Rotate 180 degrees for another person; supported translation language pairs can be swapped.

Share captions by QR/link in the app or browser. Prepare and title a QR before an event and start it later from QR history. Viewers cannot edit the source and can change their own reading guides and text size.

Caption content is end-to-end encrypted on-device before Cloudflare relays it; decryption secrets, audio and pre-existing saved histories are not sent. The browser viewer uses GitHub Pages. Providers can see connection metadata such as IP addresses. Sessions last at most eight hours; relay data is cleared on Stop, expiry or publisher interruption. Link holders can save copies that cannot be recalled. Reissue older QR codes and update both native apps.

Save received captions from the session's beginning, including later corrections. Catch-up needs an active publisher; captions not received before an offline session ends cannot be recovered.

Edit saved titles/originals, update translations or delete histories. iCloud Drive sync for saved captions and dictionaries is on by default and can be turned off; use the same Apple Account. Local saving remains available. Supported devices can create saved-caption summaries on-device with Apple Intelligence. A compatible OS/language and enabled Apple Intelligence are required; check generated results.

Share saved captions as .fumicap files through AirDrop, Mail or other system share destinations. Files preserve titles, originals, translations, speakers, times and reading guides, without audio or the full dictionary. Preview received files and save independent histories without changing the source. These files are not encrypted by this app; share only with trusted recipients.

Apple network recognition is preferred by default and may send audio/vocabulary to Apple with permission. If unavailable, prepared on-device recognition is used. Explicit on-device mode never silently switches to external recognition. Download languages in advance; recognition and translation may be incorrect.

Supported built-in microphones offer Narrow/Wide pickup. Listen through supported speaker, Bluetooth or wired outputs, and continue active transcription in the background. There are no ads, tracking, analytics or app accounts. The interface supports 50 languages/regions.

Apple Vision Pro · visionOS 26+

KF transcription mixer displays speech from the built-in microphone on Apple Vision Pro in a floating caption panel. Use it as a window in the Shared Space, and use standard visionOS controls to place and resize the panel for comfortable reading. There is one microphone input.

Use captions, text-size controls, reading guides and saved histories to follow classes, lectures and meetings at your own pace. These tools can support people who find audio alone difficult to follow, including people with hearing loss, ADHD or learning disabilities.

Enter a title at the top. Choose a language in Settings, tap Start, then Stop to finish. Set caption size and Japanese reading guides in Settings. Select original text to edit; user speech-recognition dictionaries are available.

Own and received captions save automatically with titles and final corrections. Open Saved captions to edit/delete histories or share a .fumicap file through system sharing. Preview received files and save independent copies; source files stay unchanged. Files retain text, translations and reading guides and are not encrypted by this app. iCloud Drive sync is on by default and can be turned off; local saving remains available. Sync uses the same Apple Account.

On supported devices, Apple Intelligence summarizes saved captions on-device in their original language. Summaries support reading guides. A compatible OS and language and enabled Apple Intelligence are required. Check generated results against the original.

Share captions by QR or link. Your device end-to-end encrypts caption content before Cloudflare relays it; the decryption secret is not sent to the relay. Audio and pre-existing histories are not sent. The browser viewer uses GitHub Pages. Providers can see connection metadata. Sharing ends on Stop, expiry (at most eight hours) or publisher interruption. Link holders can save copies that cannot be recalled. Reissue older QR codes and update both native apps. On Apple Vision Pro, open a QR image or enter a link without a camera. Adjust text size and reading guides in the viewer.

On-device speech recognition is selected by default. You can instead choose external recognition. On-device recognition processes speech on your device and never automatically switches to external recognition. External recognition may send audio to Apple with your permission and requires an internet connection. The first download of speech-recognition language data may also require internet access. Transcription stops when the app moves to the background.

Automatic punctuation is supported. Results depend on the OS, language and recognition method. Speech recognition and phonetic annotations can be inaccurate. This visionOS version does not create new translations; saved translations and translations received through sharing remain readable.

There are no ads, tracking, analytics or app-specific accounts. The interface supports 50 languages and regions.
